## Problem and mission

Investors currently combine broker applications, spreadsheets, dividend calculators, tax sites, calendars, disclosure portals, CBR data, banking applications, notes, and bots. Calculations disagree and the user cannot see a coherent financial position. OpenInvest unifies transparent answers about capital, real profit, dividends, tax preparation, inflation, and purchasing power.

## Product principles

Keep screens simple, target fast responses, attach sources to every figure, remain independent from brokerage sales, minimize personal data, and calculate business results on the server.

## Product modules

The long-term product map covers a public entry page, instrument catalogue, security cards, portfolio and transaction management, inflation/purchasing-power analysis, dividend calendar, tax assistant, and profile/security controls. Document 42 limits which parts enter MVP.

Portfolio analytics may include nominal, after-fee, after-tax, and after-inflation returns plus XIRR, CAGR, TWR, and money-weighted return. Only metrics explicitly included in the frozen MVP may drive first-release scope.

## Roadmap principle

Web precedes PWA, native mobile, broker imports, multicurrency, international markets, and AI. A feature that does not improve understanding of capital, returns, dividends, taxes, or purchasing power does not enter the product without explicit approval.
