Fundamental OpenInvest architecture is frozen. The authoritative decisions, priority, MVP scope, standards, and change process are defined in `SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md` and Documents 42–43.

DDD, OpenAPI-first contracts, canonical PostgreSQL data, Privacy by Design, Zero Trust, immutable financial transactions, versioned snapshots, decimal financial mathematics, event reliability, and official/permitted backend data collectors cannot change without an ADR, impact analysis, approval, and Source of Truth update.

Documentation Freeze means new ideas go to `BACKLOG_V2.md`; they do not alter MVP scope before the first release.
