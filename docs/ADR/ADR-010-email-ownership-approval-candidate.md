# ADR-010 — approved email-ownership addendum summary
**Problem / Root Cause:** Public immediate registration lacked email-ownership proof, allowing membership disclosure and pre-registration identity claiming.
**Risk:** An unverified actor could reserve an identity; retries, stale delivery leases and partial activation could harm registration liveness or existing ownership.
**Alternatives:** Reject unverified activation, persisted raw/ciphertext proofs, automatic login, email-only legacy takeover and unbounded retries.
**Decision:** Owner-approved email-only generic 202 intake creates only an intent; valid single-use generation/purpose-bound proof atomically activates a new account without a session; [owner approvals](https://github.com/AsifAbbasov/OpenInvest/blob/3e2ca51f761a1f8ea92370f666cf636b9de3b600/docs/audit/MODULE1_OWNER_CLOSEOUT_APPROVALS_V1.md).
**Rationale:** Digest-only storage, parent-first locks, fenced finite leases and per-generation attempt limits prove ownership while preserving legacy login and financial identity.
**Implementation:** [PR268](https://github.com/AsifAbbasov/OpenInvest/pull/268) MERGED at `3e2ca51f761a1f8ea92370f666cf636b9de3b600`; [complete approved addendum: API, proof/lease timing, grants, frontend, retention and rollback contracts](https://github.com/AsifAbbasov/OpenInvest/blob/3e2ca51f761a1f8ea92370f666cf636b9de3b600/docs/ADR/ADR-010-email-ownership-approval-candidate.md) remains normative; original migration-pinned ADR010 and migration 000015 unchanged.
**Verification:** Technical acceptance PASS; [post-merge CI 38758661473](https://github.com/AsifAbbasov/OpenInvest/actions/runs/38758661473) SUCCESS 10/10 on the integrated SHA; [scoped evidence matrix and historical limitations](https://github.com/AsifAbbasov/OpenInvest/blob/3e2ca51f761a1f8ea92370f666cf636b9de3b600/docs/audit/MODULE1_ARCHITECTURE_ACCEPTANCE_AND_INTEGRATION_READINESS.md).
**Limitations:** Four attempts per generation; activity-driven cleanup is not an idle retention guarantee; at-least-once SMTP cannot stop remote work; retain additive schema/evidence on rollback, never restore unverified activation; SMTP/recovery/immediate JWT revocation/deployment remain separately gated.
**Status:** Architecture addendum APPROVED and integrated; frozen proposed/pending labels are historical, not current approval status; deployment NOT AUTHORIZED; formal Module #1 closure is not newly established.
